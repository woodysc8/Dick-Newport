# Monthly transaction history

Home and the old Budget route now lead to Transactions. Navigation, Accounts, Reports and Transactions no longer mount budget cards, targets, allowances or classification previews. Existing calculation modules, correction APIs and their tests remain available for compatibility; financial records, categories, connections and saved preferences are unchanged. Account connection/refresh controls remain under Accounts.

The authenticated existing transactions endpoint accepts `view=monthly`, calendar `month`, `account` and `page`. Month boundaries use America/New_York for the default month and end-exclusive calendar dates for the read. All transaction sources participate, independently of old weekly/monthly inclusion preferences. Exact owner-scoped reads are bounded at 10,000 monthly rows; the browser receives at most 50 rows. Errors, truncated coverage and changing counts fail instead of manufacturing partial totals. Pending replacement lookups are owner-scoped and span calendar boundaries.

Posted purchases are positive, explicitly posted amounts, excluding verified financial movements. An explicit manual movement category or a consistent high-confidence modern taxonomy for internal account transfers, investment transfers or card payments supports exclusion. Broad transfer labels, legacy payment/broker text, merchant names and ambiguous Venmo descriptions do not establish an internal transfer. Uncertain posted debits stay in the provisional figure. Incoming amounts, including income/refunds/transfers, are neither new purchases nor deductions from purchases. Pending purchases remain separate. Rent, groceries and other essentials are purchases when supported, without discretionary-budget restrictions.

Provider transaction identities and pending-to-posted links suppress provable repeated records. Durable account identities group equivalent connections in the account filter. Distinct provider transaction identities are retained from every source; if multiple sources for the same durable account remain after proven deduplication, combined money figures are unavailable until their histories can be reconciled. Equal legitimate purchases are not merged by merchant/date/amount heuristics. Without durable identities or provider links, hidden duplicate connections cannot be proved; coverage remains unverified. Suppression affects the display only, never saved records.

The UI always labels the monthly figure provisional: reading transactions does not prove complete import coverage or Plaid freshness. It distinguishes the confirmed purchase subtotal from unverified debits in expandable details and withholds combined amounts for missing amounts/statuses or incompatible currencies. Transaction counts include the deduplicated posted, pending and unknown-status rows of the chosen month/account. No previous-month comparison is shown because comparable coverage is not established. These are purchase totals, not cash flow or safe-to-spend amounts.

Validation uses deterministic fixtures, mocked authenticated reads and the local production build. No live financial data was queried or modified, no migration/history repair was performed, and no Plaid refresh or deployment was requested. The branch-specific Vercel automatic-deployment block remains in place.
